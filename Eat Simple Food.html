The Lazy Cook's Thanksgiving Recipes / Menu

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

Instaread

I don't see the point in making a huge Thanksgiving dinner that takes a week to cook and several trips to the grocery store.

In fact, I like to go to the grocery store and get everything for Thanksgiving recipes and menu by at least the Friday before if possible.

I'm trying to normalize a Thanksgiving where excess is not the reason for thanks. Sometimes, I don't even make a Thanksgiving appetizer or dessert, or even a traditional Thanksgiving dinner...

Enter the lazy cook's Thanksgiving recipes and sample menu. There are choices for each category of appetizers, salads, mains, side dishes, and desserts. They start off easy and get harder in each category.

Pick an appetizer (or none), a salad (or none) or a couple side dishes and a main dish recipe and get your Thanksgiving menu wrapped up.

And a pre-made pie or a bar of chocolate and ice cream from the store go a long way if you don't like to bake.

Some of these are unconventional Thanksgiving recipes and some are more traditional Thanksgiving recipes and sample menus.

If you like a main dish and 4 sides, then knock it out. Who needs a Thanksgiving appetizer? Thanksgiving is your day to make the menu that you want to eat, not what tradition has made for you.

It doesn't get easier than rolling small pickles in salami with mustard and arugula. Stick a toothpick in it. Happy easy Thanksgiving appetizer for the lazy cook.

Cranberry Goat Cheese Crostini

These cranberry goat cheese crostini are perfect as a Thanksgiving appetizer. The cranberry orange relish is easy to make but you can also buy it pre-made at the grocery store. Crostini is just a baguette sliced and toasted. Slather goat cheese over tiny toast and top with cranberry orange relish.
