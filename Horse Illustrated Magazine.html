Equestrian Holiday Shopping List

By Holly Caccamise - November 9, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:20

Jump start your equestrian holiday shopping list with inspiration from some of our staff’s favorite brands.

Stud Muffins 

These tasty and nutritious treats are the ultimate reward. The recipe starts with grains like wheat, oats, barely and corn, and then are fortified with flaxseed and other wholesome ingredients to make sure they’re as healthy as they are enjoyable.

Shop for various prices depending on size at chewy.com.

Taborton Equine Books

Explore nearly 1,500 titles for horse lovers! Buy three or more and save 20 percent—perfect for everyone on your list.

Shop at readriderepeat.com.

Mare Goods Fancy Horses Blanket

Soft and cozy, our throw blanket features signature GG Design with fancy horses in a cheerful design—perfect for adding touch of equestrian flair to your décor.

Shop for $90.00 at maregoods.com.

Cowgirl Magic Ultra Hydrating Cream 

Fresh, aromatic scent and age-defying properties for the entire body, face, hands and feet. Deeply hydrates to firm skin and fight the appearance of wrinkles.

Shop for $12.99 at cowboymagic.com.

Charles Owen Kylo

The Kylo exudes quality, style and comfort with an easy-to-fit precision dial-fit system. MIPS technology available to protect you with the very best in helmet safety. Available in both navy and black with a high-quality matte or gloss finish.

Shop starting at $190.00 at charlesowen.com.

German Minty Muffins

Each muffin is a decadent blend of whole grains, bran, wheat flour,  soybean meal, molasses, corn dehydrated alfalfa meal, vitamins and more.  Made with a delicious peppermint candy.

Price varies by retailer; learn more at equusmagnificus.com.

Luna Ring Horse Toy

The Luna Ring is expertly designed to alleviate boredom and reduce stress for your horse, whether during trailering or stall rest. This innovative treat puzzle is crafted to provide endless entertainment and mental stimulation.

Shop for $32.99 at petkore.com.

Groovy Ruby II Saddle Pad

Shop saddles and accessories just for kids and ponies! This pad features a vibrant tie-dye pattern. Each pad is trimmed with lace ruffles in several color options.

Shop for $56.50 at rubyroosaddles.com.

ShowSheen Detangler Gel

Designed specifically to tackle the toughest knots, this detangler gel combines Argan Oil of Morocco with deep conditioning ingredients for a high-gloss, silky-soft finish that lasts for days. 

Shop for $13.99 at absorbine.com.
