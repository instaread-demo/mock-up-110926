Now that I have harvested my (potentially cursed!) elderberries, it’s time to use them for a little hobbit kitchen magic! So put on the kettle, or prepare your pipe, and sit back and relax. I will show you how to turn even a small harvest into something healthy and tasty to enjoy all through the winter.❄️ Elderberry syrup can be frozen and used in sauces, teas, and even just enjoyed on its own!

Want a printer-friendly version of the recipe? Click here.

A note on elderberries: Correct identification and preparation are essential. Raw or improperly prepared elderberries and other parts of the plant can cause illness. Never eat unidentified wild berries. Use good hobbit sense – never forage from roadsides, places that could have been sprayed by chemicals, etc. Be sure to use low and slow heat when cooking to render the berries safe from toxins.
