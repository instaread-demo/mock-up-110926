What Is Ice Cream Hair


@charliegray248

The ice cream cut hair is the new sensation on TikTok and it’s time to understand what this haircut is all about! Similar to the hairstyles you see on cartoon characters, this ice cream hair focuses on a voluminous and fluffy style with ends that flip outwards. Popular London barber and educator, Charles Gray, has created a masterpiece from this trend, and talking about one of his creations, he says:

This model‘s hair is naturally curly so what I did is blow dry the roots through to the mid-length straight and then used the brush to just slightly curl the ends up and use the natural curl in the hair to create this creative styling.

How To Get Ice Cream Hair

@cameronrohpovs the long awaited hair tutorial has arrived 🫡 #fyp #hairtutorial #cameronroh ♬ Back On 74 – Sped Up Version – Jungle

  1. The first thing to note is that the ice cream cone cut is more of a lengthy cut as you need to flip your hair upward. So grow your hair up to at least 4 inches or till your ends stop below your eyes.
  2. The next step is to have a sample of what you want for reference and incorporate layers in the front of your hair to shed excess weight.
  3. For the volume and texture, blowdrying is needed so don’t forget to apply a heat protectant to reduce the possibility of frizz and heat damage that may occur.
  4. While blowdrying, use a rounded brush to create the flip-end effect. Repeat the process
  5. Finally, keep your ice cream cut in place with any volumizing hairspray product of your choice. It is advisable to spray the underside of your hair so that your locs don’t weigh down and fall flat.
