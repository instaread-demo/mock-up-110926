The AstroLibrary free Compatibility Report has been updated to give you more, whether or not you know your birth times.

Change 1: Works both with or without birth times

Now you can enter birth times for one or both partners to get a reading of your synastry aspects, including aspects to the Moon, Ascendant, Midheaven, Vertex, and Part of Fortune. It also gives you a list of house overlays if either birth time is given.

Even if you don’t know the birth times, you can still get a detailed compatibility reading based on Sun and planetary aspects.

Change 2: Synastry Chart Images and Aspect Grid

Now the report includes synastry chart images and an aspect grid.

Change 3: Includes a list of Synastry Aspects

Now you get a list format of all your synastry aspects with orbs, so you can see these at a glance.
