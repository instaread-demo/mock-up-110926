Planning a Vegetarian Christmas Dinner

Putting together a vegetarian Christmas menu doesn't have to be complicated. I like to start by choosing the main dish, then build the rest of the meal around it.

A simple way to plan your menu is to choose:

  • One vegetarian main dish: such as Mushroom Wellington, nut roast or stuffed butternut squash.
  • Two or three vegetable sides: perhaps Brussels sprouts, roasted parsnips and cauliflower cheese.
  • A potato dish: crispy roast potatoes or creamy mashed potatoes.
  • Stuffing and gravy: the essential Christmas trimmings!
  • A festive dessert: ideally something you can prepare ahead.

You can add a starter, extra sides or homemade sauces depending on how many people you're feeding.

Tips for an Easier Christmas Dinner

Prepare what you can in advance. Check each recipe for make-ahead and storage instructions. Sauces, some desserts and certain vegetable dishes can often be prepared before Christmas Day.

Plan your oven space. Work out which dishes need the oven and at what temperature. An air fryer or slow cooker can be useful for preparing sides without taking up valuable oven space.

Write a cooking schedule. Once you've chosen your recipes, note their preparation and cooking times so everything is ready to serve together.

Make a shopping list. Check the ingredients for all your chosen recipes and combine them into one list. Don't forget to check what you already have in the cupboards.

Keep things manageable. You don't need to make every dish from scratch or serve lots of different recipes. A delicious main, a few favourite sides and a lovely dessert can make a wonderful Christmas meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do vegetarians eat for Christmas dinner?

There are lots of delicious vegetarian alternatives to a traditional roast. Mushroom Wellington, nut roast, stuffed butternut squash and savoury vegetable pies all make wonderful Christmas main dishes. Serve them with roast potatoes, seasonal vegetables, meat-free stuffing and vegetarian gravy.

Can I make a vegetarian Christmas dinner ahead of time?

Yes! Many parts of a vegetarian Christmas dinner can be prepared in advance. Choose recipes with suitable make-ahead instructions, prepare vegetables where possible, and make sauces or desserts ahead to save time on Christmas Day.

What Christmas side dishes are vegetarian?

Many traditional Christmas sides can be vegetarian, including roast potatoes, Brussels sprouts, roasted parsnips, cauliflower cheese, red cabbage and mashed potatoes. Check ingredients such as animal fat, meat-based stock and cheese made with animal rennet when cooking for vegetarians.

Can I make a vegan Christmas dinner?

Absolutely! There are plenty of plant-based Christmas mains, sides and desserts to choose from. Try a vegan nut roast or stuffed squash with roasted vegetables, vegan stuffing and dairy-free gravy.
